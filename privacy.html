Ares privacy policy

Ares is a private app used by one household. When the account owner signs in with Google, Ares reads the Nest devices they choose to share through Google's Smart Device Management API: device status, thermostat settings, and camera and doorbell events.

That data stays on the household's own computers. It is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. No one outside the household has access to it.

The account owner can remove Ares's access at any time in their Google account settings or the Google Home app.
